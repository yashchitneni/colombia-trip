Know Before You Go

Pro Tips 🧠

🛂No visa needed. US citizens get 90 days on arrival. Just bring your passport.
💱Currency: Colombian Peso (COP). ~4,200 COP = $1 USD. Cards accepted widely, but carry some cash.
📱Uber works everywhere in Colombia. Download it before you go. Super cheap.
💃Cali = Salsa Capital of the World. Take a lesson. Go to a salsoteca. You won't regret it.
💧Tap water: Stick to bottled water in most places. Bogotá tap is fine, but play it safe elsewhere.
⚡Book flights NOW. Prices are climbing due to geopolitics and seasonal demand. Earlier = cheaper.
🌡️Weather: Cali is warm year-round (~85°F). Cartagena is hot and humid. Pack light!
